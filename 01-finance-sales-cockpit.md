# Small-Business Finance & Sales Cockpit

## What it is
A browser-based demo that turns order, customer, product, and receivables data into a compact operating view: sales trends, cash collection, overdue invoices, and stalled orders. Use synthetic or public data and label it clearly as a portfolio demo.

## Why build it
It makes the value of a data service immediately visible to a business owner: less spreadsheet chasing, faster answers, and a shared view of operations. It extends the practical story already present in the Omie ERP case without exposing client information.

## Skills it highlights
- Data modeling and KPI definition
- Python/SQL ingestion and transformation
- Dashboard design for non-technical users
- Data quality checks and clear business communication

## Suggested deliverable
A live dashboard with a short walkthrough, a simple architecture diagram, sample data, and a README explaining assumptions and how to run it. Include drill-downs for overdue invoices and sales by customer/product.

## Evidence of quality
Show reconciliation checks between source totals and dashboard totals, document metric definitions, and include a few realistic questions the dashboard answers. Avoid claiming business impact from synthetic data.
