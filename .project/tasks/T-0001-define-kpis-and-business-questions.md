Define the small-business audience, the decisions the cockpit supports, and the metric definitions.

Acceptance criteria:

- A concise target-user statement and 5--7 realistic business questions.
- Definitions for sales, collected cash, overdue receivables, stalled orders, and the time window each uses.
- A documented list of dashboard filters and drill-down dimensions.
